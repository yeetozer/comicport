# Mac App Store Screenshot Plan

Apple currently accepts 1–10 Mac screenshots. Use one consistent 16:10 size:
- 1280 × 800
- 1440 × 900
- 2560 × 1600
- 2880 × 1800

Recommended ComicPort set: 6 screenshots at 2880 × 1800.

1. Library
   Caption: “Your comic library, organized.”
   Show cover grid, progress, sidebar and search.

2. Reader
   Caption: “A focused reader built for Mac.”
   Show a self-created/licensed sample comic, page controls and progress.

3. Smart Collections
   Caption: “Collections that organize themselves.”
   Show built-in + custom dynamic shelves.

4. Comic Enhance
   Caption: “Tune every page without changing the file.”
   Show the reader settings with Clarity/Vivid/Ink/Soft.

5. Page Language Tools
   Caption: “Recognize, copy and read page text.”
   Show OCR output. Only show Translation if tested on the shipping OS.

6. Reading Insights / Sources & Sync
   Caption: “Track your reading. Keep control of your files.”
   Show statistics or the local-first Sources & Sync screen.

IMPORTANT:
Do not use unlicensed Marvel/DC/other commercial comic pages as App Store marketing
art. Use original artwork, licensed artwork, or clearly public-domain sample content.
Screenshots cannot contain an alpha channel.
