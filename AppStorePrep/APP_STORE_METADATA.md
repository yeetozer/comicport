Description:
ComicPort is a local-first comic reader and library for Mac.

Bring your own PDF and comic archive files and keep your reading experience organized in one private, offline-focused app.

READ YOUR WAY
• PDF, CBZ, CBR/RAR, CB7/7z, CBT/TAR and ZIP support
• Single-page, double-page and vertical reading
• Right-to-left manga reading
• Auto Crop, manual crop, Fit Width and Fit Page
• Non-destructive Comic Enhance presets
• Remembered reading position and next-issue navigation

ORGANIZE A LARGE LIBRARY
• Automatic covers and reading progress
• Reading, To Read and Completed statuses
• Favorites and star ratings
• Series grouping and reading orders
• Smart Collections that update automatically
• Local Source folders for finding newly added comics

LOCAL INTELLIGENCE
• ComicInfo.xml metadata support
• Offline title and character organization
• Local Smart Search with text recognition
• Page Language Tools with OCR, copy and Read Aloud
• Apple system translation when supported and downloaded on the Mac
• Reading recommendations and comic-calendar events

TRACK YOUR READING
• Daily goal, streaks, pages and reading time
• Reading history and journal
• Achievements and profile progress
• Backup and restore for your ComicPort data

PRIVACY-FIRST
ComicPort does not provide or download comic books. You import files you own or are authorized to use. Reading, OCR, organization and analysis are designed to work locally on your Mac. ComicPort has no advertising account system and no ComicPort analytics server.

Optional Source and Sync folders are folders you explicitly choose. Comic files are not uploaded by ComicPort Sync; it stores lightweight reading-state metadata only.

ComicPort is not affiliated with comic publishers or rights holders. Product and character names belong to their respective owners.
