# Content Rights / Trademark Positioning — Release Draft

ComicPort should be positioned as a reader/tool, not as a content provider.

App Store listing and review notes should make clear that:
- comic files are supplied by the user;
- ComicPort does not provide a comic-download catalog;
- users are responsible for importing files they own or are authorized to use;
- ComicPort is not affiliated with publishers or character/property owners.

For screenshots and promotional assets, use only:
- original ComicPort-created sample artwork;
- public-domain artwork where its status has been verified; or
- artwork you have explicit marketing rights to use.

Avoid publisher logos and character artwork in the App Store screenshot set unless
you have the necessary rights.

The built-in metadata/recommendation database should be reviewed before public
release for any images, long copyrighted text, publisher logos, or other assets
that should not be redistributed. Titles, creator names and factual metadata should
still be reviewed as part of the final content-rights check.

This is a release checklist, not legal advice.
